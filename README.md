Bright Car Sales Analysis

 📖 Project Overview

This project analyzes car sales data from **Bright Motors** to uncover insights that improve sales performance, optimize inventory management, and support business growth.

Using data analytics and visualization techniques, the project explores revenue drivers, customer preferences, and regional sales performance to provide actionable business recommendations.


🎯 Project Objective

The primary objective of this project is to:

- Identify top-performing car models.
- Analyze pricing and sales patterns.
- Evaluate regional sales performance.
- Provide data-driven recommendations to increase profitability and operational efficiency.

❓ Business Questions

- Which car models generate the highest revenue?
- Which regions achieve the strongest sales performance?
- How do pricing strategies influence sales?
- What are the monthly and yearly sales trends?
- Which customer segments contribute the most to revenue?
- What recommendations can improve sales and inventory management?
